hello! my name is aron / scacha.

i want to present you my first work — the mixtape sonder.

for me, it is a big step into electronic soul music. sonder works as an invitation to go deep inside someone's story. it is a repetitive experience that becomes more recognisible each time you face it. you may find something yours in these sounds. something that wasn't able to be named deep inside you until this moment.

i've faced a lot of moments of joy, yearning and grief. i have my own story for this work, but you can (preferably MUST) have your own story to lay over these recordings.

i don't want to be seen as a closed genius musician or something like that. i want to be (already) clear in my art and honest with you.
